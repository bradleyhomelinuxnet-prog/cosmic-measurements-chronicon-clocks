# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 7 November 2026

The first release. It joins the Chronicon clock, Petrie's measurements and the book *Cosmic Measurements* into one offline instrument.

- **Clock:** the Chronicon engine, now reading its events from discovery files. Annus Mundi leads and the Hebrew count is minimised.
- **Plates:** four survey plates drawn live from Petrie's figures, a sortable table of every measurement, and a table that recomputes each relationship the book draws.
- **Discoveries:** an in-app form saves drafts that appear everywhere at once and exports files to commit. It ships with 19 discoveries.
- **Timeline:** dated discoveries over the 138-year grid, with a palindrome fold around any axis.
- **Book:** the full text with live dates and measurements. Each section links to its mirror.
- **Also:** the 19 · 138 index, a unit converter with the working shown, and the films.
- **No outside loads:** fonts are stored in the repo, and nothing loads from outside until a film is played.
- **Corrections to the book:** the perimeter ratio is stated as 3.14 and the royal cubit as 0.523 m. The claim that the Upper Chamber encodes pi is removed.
